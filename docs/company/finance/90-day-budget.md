## 2. Order of spend (don't pay for anything before it unblocks something)

1. **Week 1 (by 1 Oct), about $70:** name check (free), domain, Workspace, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, virtual mailbox. This unblocks interviews and outreach.
2. **By 8 Oct, $100–1,500:** entity, Stripe account, engagement letter/ToS (lawyer if you can afford it), insurance quotes requested.
3. **By 15 Oct, about $80:** hosting + LLM credits for the S5 readiness check, landing page.
4. **Before the first paid pack (target late Oct):** insurance bound, bookkeeping set up, accountant consult if the comfortable budget allows.
5. **Defer to 2027:** trademark (unless the name search shows a conflict, in which case rename *now*), paid Sales Navigator if free outreach works, and any ads.

## 3. Cash in against cash out (base case)

- Base case: **~4.9 packs by mid-December**, mostly at the $495 founding price, is about **$2,330 net**. That covers the minimum budget 2–3.5 times and roughly half of the comfortable budget.
- Hitting just the **north star of 3 packs** is about $1,430 net, which covers the minimum budget on either path.
- Conservative case (2.6 packs, ~$1,240 net): the founder funds about **$3,550** at the lowest point (see `model-summary.md` §5). **Plan to have $3,500–5,000 available** to cover the comfortable budget plus a slow first season. That assumes the founder takes no salary.
