## 0. Two questions that decide this

1. **Where do you live and pay tax today?** Your country of residence, not your citizenship, drives banking access (Mercury, Stripe) and home-country foreign-exchange law.
2. **Will you raise venture money in the next 18 months?** If yes, US investors almost always expect a **Delaware C-corp**. If you're bootstrapping to 3–10 customers first, an LLC or a local company is cheaper and simpler.

Tell the legal-ops agent both answers and this doc will narrow to one path.

## 1. The three paths

| | **(1) US-resident founder: Delaware C-corp (or home-state LLC)** | **(2) Non-US founder (e.g. Nepal) selling to US via a US entity (Atlas / Firstbase)** | **(3) Nepal Pvt. Ltd. selling to US customers directly** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Formation cost** | Stripe Atlas **$500** one-time, which covers the Delaware filing, EIN, legal templates, 83(b) filing and 1 year of registered agent. After that the agent costs **$100/yr** ([Stripe Atlas](https://stripe.com/atlas); price per [Rho review](https://www.rho.co/blog/stripe-atlas-review), 3P). Firstbase **$399** + registered agent **$299/yr** ([Firstbase pricing](https://www.firstbase.io/pricing), 3P via [Rho](https://www.rho.co/blog/firstbase-pricing)). You can also DIY through a home-state LLC. | Same vendors and prices as (1). Firstbase says it supports founders in 191 countries (3P). **Nepal residents are not guaranteed a bank account or Stripe approval.** See the Banking row. | Government registration fee at the **Office of the Company Registrar** (ocr.gov.np) scales with authorized capital, from about **NPR 1,000 to NPR 10,500+** for typical small capital (3P: [CompanySewa](https://companysewa.com/blog/company-registration-fees-nepal/), [Merokalam](https://merokalam.com/blog/register-company-nepal-ocr/)). Add local lawyer or agent fees, PAN/VAT registration and ward registration. **FLAG:** confirm the current fee slab with OCR. |
| **Time to form** | 1–3 business days to incorporate. EIN is online and fast for US residents. | Incorporation takes 1–3 days. The **EIN takes longer** for foreign founders: by phone (+1 267-941-1099) or fax, and no SSN/ITIN is needed ([IRS SS-4 instructions, rev. Dec 2025](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iss4)). Firstbase quotes 8–12 business days (3P). | **FLAG:** usually quoted as days to a few weeks, plus separate IT-industry registration if you want IT incentives or outward-investment rights. Not verified. |
| **Annual upkeep (government)** | C-corp: Delaware franchise tax, minimum **$175** (authorized-shares method) or **$400** (assumed-par-value method), plus **$50** annual report, due **March 1** ([Delaware Division of Corporations](https://corp.delaware.gov/frtax/)). LLC: **$300/yr due June 1**, no annual report ([Delaware LLC tax](https://corp.delaware.gov/alt-entitytaxinstructions/)). Home-state registration is also needed where you operate. **California charges $800/yr minimum** ([CA FTB, corporations](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/business/types/corporations/index.html), [CA FTB, LLCs](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/business/types/limited-liability-company/index.html)). | Delaware fees as in (1). A **foreign-owned single-member LLC** must file **pro forma Form 1120 + Form 5472** every year even with zero revenue. The penalty is **$25,000** per failure ([IRS i5472](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i5472)). A C-corp files Form 1120 (plus 5472 if 25%+ foreign-owned). Budget **$500–2,000/yr** for a US tax preparer (estimate). | Annual return to OCR, audited accounts (a Nepali auditor is required), income tax and VAT returns with the Inland Revenue Department. **FLAG:** confirm deadlines and costs locally. |
| **Tax basics** | C-corp: 21% federal corporate tax plus state tax, then dividend tax. Eligible for **QSBS (IRC §1202)**: for stock issued after July 4, 2025, 50/75/100% gain exclusion at 3/4/5 years, with a $15M cap ([Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.com/insights/alerts/tax/2025/insights/explaining-enhanced-section-1202-benefits), [Baker Tilly](https://www.bakertilly.com/insights/changes-to-section-1202-qualified-small-business)). File **83(b) within 30 days** of receiving founder stock (Atlas does this). LLC: pass-through to your personal return. | **There is no US–Nepal income tax treaty** ([IRS treaties A–Z](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/international-businesses/united-states-income-tax-treaties-a-to-z)), so dividends from a C-corp to a Nepal-resident owner face **30% US withholding** ([PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/united-states/corporate/withholding-taxes)). Nepal also taxes its residents on worldwide income. **FLAG, high risk:** (a) Nepal may treat a US company that is managed and controlled from Nepal as Nepal-resident (Income Tax Act 2058). (b) Whether a US single-member LLC with no US office has US tax exposure depends on facts. A cross-border accountant must model this. | Standard corporate rate plus **IT-export incentives**. The FY 2082/83 budget (May 30, 2025) gave a 75% rebate on IT-export income, which was widely reported as about 5% effective. The **FY 2083/84 budget reportedly cut it to 50%** and added a sweat-equity exemption. Separately, there is a **5% final tax** on individuals' IT-export income through banks, up to NPR 4M (3P: [Khatapana](https://khatapana.com/blogs/496/digital-nepal-budget-2082-5-tax-rate-on-it-exports), [Law Alpine 2083/84](https://lawalpine.com/blog/budget-2083-84-tax-changes-in-nepal), [b360](https://www.b360nepal.com/detail/29119/nepals-it-budget-208384)). **FLAG:** sources conflict. Confirm the current rates and eligibility (IT-industry registration, NRB channel rules) with a Nepali tax advisor. |
| **Fundraising fit** | **Best.** A Delaware C-corp is the default for US angels, accelerators and VCs (SAFEs, standard docs). An LLC usually has to convert before a priced round. | **Good for US investors** in a Delaware C-corp. However, **FLAG, critical:** under Nepal's **Act Restricting Investment Abroad, 2021 BS (1964)**, Nepali citizens generally may not invest abroad without a government exemption ([summary: CompanyNP](https://companynp.com/act-restricting-investment-abroad-1964/), [b360](https://www.b360nepal.com/detail/23525/unveiling-outward-investment-a-nepali-law-perspective-2024-Sep-27-637000)). A Nepal-resident citizen holding Delaware shares may breach it. The **2025 amendments** (economic-environment omnibus act, March 2025; FERA §10A; NRB bylaws amended June 2, 2025) opened outward investment for **Nepali IT companies**. They did not clearly open it for **individuals** (3P: [Pioneer Law](https://pioneerlaw.com/resource/legal-pathway-for-nepali-companies-to-invest-abroad-through-technology-transfer/), [Infinity](https://infinitynp.com/insights/abroad-investment-nepal-it-companies-fera-nrb)). | **Weak for US VCs.** Most will ask you to "flip" into a Delaware parent, which brings back the outward-investment question above. It works fine for bootstrapping and for Nepal or regional investors. Nepal's FITTA governs inbound foreign investment ([UNCTAD: FITTA 2019](https://investmentpolicy.unctad.org/investment-laws/laws/313/nepal-foreign-investment-and-technology-transfer-act-2019-2075-)). |
| **Payments (Stripe)** | Stripe US is fully available. | A **US entity can hold a Stripe US account.** Stripe is **not available to Nepal-registered businesses** ([Stripe global](https://stripe.com/global)). **FLAG:** Stripe may still review where the business is actually operated. Stripe's Atlas FAQ for **India** says Atlas isn't designed for Indian residents because of RBI outward-investment rules ([Stripe support](https://support.stripe.com/questions/faq-for-indian-founders-using-stripe-atlas)). Nepal's rules are similar in spirit, and Stripe has published no Nepal-specific guidance. | **No Stripe.** Options: SWIFT wire or ACH-to-SWIFT invoices, **Payoneer**, or a merchant of record such as **Paddle**, which third-party reports list as supporting Nepal sellers ([Paddle supported countries](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/intro-to-paddle/which-countries-are-supported-by-paddle); **FLAG**, verify at signup). Card subscriptions for US SMBs are clumsy without Stripe or a merchant of record. |
| **Banking** | Mercury, Relay, a local bank, and so on. | **Mercury lists Nepal as a prohibited country, based on the founder's residence, not citizenship** ([Mercury prohibited countries](https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/28771710754580-Prohibited-countries); news: [The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/fintech/news/mercury-stops-serving-startups-in-several-countries)). Atlas's default bank may therefore fail. **FLAG:** check whether Wise Business, Airwallex, Relay or others onboard Nepal-resident owners before you pay for formation. | Nepali commercial bank (NPR plus a foreign-currency account if eligible). Inbound export proceeds go through **NRB rules** (documentation, conversion) ([NRB Foreign Exchange Management Dept.](https://www.nrb.org.np/departments/fxm/)). |
| **US customer trust** | Highest. US contracts, US courts, easy BAA and insurance. | High. A US entity signs contracts. Insurers and clinics may still ask where data and staff sit. | Lower for HIPAA clinics and FTC-regulated tax preparers, who must vet service providers. US cyber and E&O insurance for a Nepal entity is harder to buy (**FLAG**). |
| **Beneficial-ownership report (CTA)** | **Not required** for US-formed entities. The final rule is effective **Aug 14, 2026** ([Federal Register 2026-16576](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/14/2026-16576/beneficial-ownership-information-reporting-requirement-revision), [FinCEN BOI](https://www.fincen.gov/boi)). | Same: a US-formed entity is exempt. | Only if it registers to do business in a US state. |

## 2. Foreign exchange and receiving payments: option 2 vs. option 3

| Question | (2) US entity, founder in Nepal | (3) Nepal Pvt. Ltd. |
|---|---|---|
| Where customer money lands | USD in a US bank or Stripe balance. No Nepal FX rules apply at this stage. | Directly in Nepal as export proceeds, subject to NRB documentation and conversion rules. Export documents now need specific fields under the Unified Forex Circular amendments (3P: [PKF flash alert](https://pkf.trunco.com.np/files/publications/1775657534_Flash%20Alert%20on%20NRB%20Amendments%20to%20Unified%20Forex%20Circular%202081%202.pdf)). |
| How the founder gets paid | The US company pays the founder as a **contractor** (IT services exported from Nepal), with the payment arriving through a bank or Payoneer. This may qualify the founder for the 5% final tax on individual IT-export income (**FLAG**). Dividends face 30% US withholding and Nepal tax. | Salary or dividends inside Nepal under ordinary Nepali tax. |
| Getting money **out** to pay US vendors (AWS, LLM APIs, insurance) | Easy: pay in USD from the US account. | Harder: each outward payment needs NRB-permitted channels and limits. Card limits for SaaS spending are a known pain (**FLAG**, confirm with your bank). |
| Legal risk | **Outward-investment law** (owning the US company) and **tax residence** of the US company. This is the biggest unknown. | Lowest home-country legal risk. The main risk is commercial: US buyers' trust, and payment friction. |
| Recurring card billing | Stripe Billing works. | A merchant of record (Paddle, if available) or manual invoices. |

**A compliant-looking structure that a lawyer should evaluate (FLAG, not verified):** a Nepal Pvt. Ltd. registered as an **IT industry**, which then makes an **NRB-approved outward investment** into a **US subsidiary** under the 2025 FERA §10A route. The US subsidiary contracts with US customers and holds Stripe. The Nepal company does the engineering and invoices the US company. This may give you Stripe and US trust while staying within Nepal law, but it is the most expensive setup and US VCs may later want a Delaware parent on top.

## 3. Recommendation by founder location (pending your answers)

| If you… | Start with | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Live in the US and plan to raise** | **Delaware C-corp via Stripe Atlas** ($500), register as a foreign corporation in your home state, file the 83(b) | Investor default and QSBS. Atlas bundles the 83(b) and IP assignment. |
| **Live in the US and are bootstrapping** | **Single-member LLC in your home state** (cheaper than Delaware plus a home-state registration). Convert to a C-corp before raising. | Lowest cost and pass-through tax. |
| **Live in Nepal (Nepali citizen)** | **Get a Nepali lawyer's written opinion first** on the Act Restricting Investment Abroad and FERA §10A. Until then, **do customer discovery without an entity**: no sales are needed for interviews. If you must invoice early, use a Nepal Pvt. Ltd. (or sole proprietorship) with Payoneer or Paddle. | Forming a Delaware company from Nepal without clearance may breach Nepali law. Mercury won't bank you, and Stripe's position is unclear. |
| **Are a Nepali citizen living outside Nepal (NRN)** | Probably path (1) or (2) under your country of residence's rules | NRNs may invest earnings made abroad (3P: [CompanyNP](https://companynp.com/act-restricting-investment-abroad-1964/)). **FLAG:** confirm your specific status. |

## 4. Needs a real lawyer or accountant
1. **Nepal outward-investment clearance** before any Nepal resident owns US shares (Nepali corporate or FX lawyer).
2. **Tax-residence and PE analysis** for a US company run from Nepal, and the choice between LLC and C-corp for a non-US owner (cross-border CPA).
3. **Current Nepal IT-export tax rate and eligibility** (Nepali tax advisor, because the sources conflict).
4. **Home-state registration** and sales-tax treatment of SaaS for a US founder (state CPA). SaaS taxability varies by state and is **not covered here**.
