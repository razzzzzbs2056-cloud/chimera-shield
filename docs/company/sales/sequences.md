# Sales Outreach Sequences: PTIN renewal window 2026

**Owner:** head-of-sales · **Date:** 2026-09-24 · **Status:** ready to send once gates G1–G4 and G8 in `playbook.md` §2 are cleared
**Follows:** `.claude/skills/outreach-writing/SKILL.md` (under 100 words, one ask, real sender, opt-out). Discovery (research) outreach is in `docs/company/interviews/recruiting-kit.md`; do not send both to the same person in the same month. If someone already did a discovery interview, use the warm sequence W1 instead.

## Rules before you hit send
- **Cadence:** Touch 1 → +3 days → +5 days → +7 days. **Stop on any reply or opt-out**, including "not now" (move to `nurture-may`).
- **Touch 1 is plain text with no links.** Links (the Readiness Check URL) only from touch 3.
- **The personal first line must be true and specific.** If you can't write one in 2 minutes, skip the prospect or send LinkedIn only. Line bank at the bottom.
- **No stats, no customer claims, no findings about their domain.** We haven't looked at their domain and we say nothing about it.
- **Placeholders stay visible until real:** `[FOUNDER_NAME]`, `[DOMAIN]`, `[POSTAL_ADDRESS]`, `[READINESS_CHECK_URL]`, `[PACK_PRICE]`, `[WATCH_PRICE]`, `[FIRM]`, `[FIRST_NAME]`, `[PERSONAL_LINE]`.
- **Dates:** "PTINs expire Dec 31" and "renewal opens mid-October" are per IRS guidance; re-check irs.gov when renewal opens and update if needed. "Last intake Dec 4" is our own capacity limit, so it's true.
- US business addresses only. Log every send in `pipeline.csv` (`sequence`, `touch_step`, `last_touch_date`).

Standard signature and footer (used in every email below, counted in the word limit):
```
[FOUNDER_NAME], Founder, ChimeraShield
[FOUNDER_NAME]@[DOMAIN] · [POSTAL_ADDRESS]

If this isn't relevant, just reply "no" and I won't follow up.
```

## Sequence L: LinkedIn (4 touches; run alongside or instead of email, never both on the same day)

**L1. Connection note (under 300 characters)**
> Hi [FIRST_NAME], [SHORT_PERSONAL_LINE, max 80 characters]. I'm the founder of ChimeraShield: we help small tax and CPA firms with the written security plan the W-12 asks about at PTIN renewal. Glad to connect. [FOUNDER_NAME]

**L2. After they accept (+1–2 days)**
> Thanks for connecting, [FIRST_NAME]. Straight to it: I'm offering 5–50 person firms a done-with-you WISP before renewal. One 60-minute call, then within 7 business days you get a plan that describes your firm, a one-page evidence report, and your top 5 fixes in plain English. Worth 15 minutes to see if it fits [FIRM]? If not relevant, just say so and I won't follow up.

**L3. Resource (+5 days)**
> [FIRST_NAME], if a call is too much during renewal season, our free WISP & Safeguards Readiness Check takes about 10 minutes: 12 questions plus a public-records email security check (only with your OK). You'll get your top 3 gaps before you sign the W-12. [READINESS_CHECK_URL]

**L4. Closing the loop (+7 days)**
> Last note from me, [FIRST_NAME]. I'm taking final intake calls on Dec 4 so plans land before the holidays and tax season. If a WISP for [FIRM] is on the list, reply and I'll hold a slot. Otherwise, best of luck this season.

**Between touches:** leave 1–2 genuine comments on their posts. No pitch in comments.

## Personal first-line bank (only if true and checked)
| Trigger | Example line |
|---|---|
| New office / move | "Congrats on the new [CITY] office; I saw the announcement on your site." |
| Hiring | "Saw [FIRM] is hiring a [ROLE] for the season." |
| Directory | "Your NAEA listing says [FIRM] focuses on [SPECIALTY] in [CITY]." |
| LinkedIn post | "Your post on [TOPIC] last week, especially [POINT], stuck with me." |
| Event | "Saw you're speaking at / attending [EVENT]." |
| Software change | "Noticed [FIRM] now offers a client portal for document uploads." |
| Referral | "[NAME] suggested I get in touch." (only with the referrer's permission) |

**Banned first lines:** anything about their domain, DMARC, website security, "I noticed your email setup", breach news about a named peer, flattery without specifics.

## Word-count check
All email bodies above are under 100 words including the signature and opt-out line. If you add a personal line longer than 15 words, cut elsewhere.
