## 1. Words: what we say and never say in Australia

| Never say (AU copy, calls, invoices) | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "WISP" | "information security policy", "written security policy" |
| "certified", "certification" | "written for your practice", "checked against what we can see" |
| "compliant", "compliance pack", "makes you compliant" | "structured around the TFN Rule and TPB guidance", "helps you show what's in place" |
| "TPB-approved", "ATO-endorsed" | "independent; built on the TPB's and ATO's published guidance" |
| "Essential Eight compliant", "Essential Eight assessment" | "Essential Eight snapshot" (we don't run the ACSC assessment process) |
| "The small-business privacy exemption ends in December 2026" (or any date) | Say nothing about future reform. It has **not** been removed and no date exists. |
| "guaranteed", "you won't be breached", "continuous monitoring" | "a written plan, evidence and a short fix list" |

"TPB & TFN ready" is part of the product name. If asked, explain it plainly: "It means the Pack is built around your TFN and TPB obligations. It isn't an endorsement by either body, and you stay responsible for your obligations."

## 2. Pilot offer

### 2.1 What the practice gets
**Client Data Security Pack (TPB & TFN ready)**, concierge, delivered within 7 business days of a 60-minute intake:
1. **Information security policy** written for the practice, structured around the TFN Rule 2015, APP 11 and the TPB's confidentiality and quality-management expectations.
2. **Data breach response plan**: who does what in the first 72 hours; the NDB 30-day assessment; telling the ATO straight away if Online services for agents access is compromised; the check on whether it's a significant Code breach to report to the TPB within 30 days.
3. **One-page Evidence Report** in Essential Eight Maturity Level 1 order (a **snapshot**, not an assessment). Each item is marked **Verified / Owner-stated / Gap**, including "each staff member uses their own myID with MFA".
4. **Fix-First 5**: the five fixes to do first, each saying who does it (often the practice's IT provider).

## 3. Who we target

- **Practice:** Australian tax agent or accounting practice, **5–50 staff**, independent (not a franchise or top-100 firm). Registered tax agent on the TPB register.
- **Buyer:** principal, owner, managing partner or director. **Helper:** practice manager or operations manager (email them only if the practice's site shows they run operations or IT).
- **Priority (work first):** 5–15 staff; founder can meet in person (same city); no IT provider named on their site; warm path; owner active on LinkedIn; no mention of Practice Protect on their site (not a disqualifier, but a likely "already covered" objection).
- **Park:** sole practitioners (lighter tier later); BAS-agent-only businesses (possible cheaper tier later).

### 3.1 Prospect sources (Spam Act rules apply exactly as for the US; there is no CAN-SPAM)

| Source | What it gives | Can we email addresses from it? | How to use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TPB public register** ([tpb.gov.au/public-register](https://www.tpb.gov.au/public-register)) | Confirms a practitioner or company is a registered tax agent; name, registration number and business details | **No. Research only.** It's a statutory register for consumers to check registration, not an address published for marketing (conservative reading; legal-ops to confirm) | Confirm registration and the practice name, then go to the practice's own website |
| **CPA Australia "Find a CPA"** | Public-practice CPAs by location and services | **Only if** the directory's terms allow commercial contact and legal-ops clears it; otherwise research only | Find practices, then the website |

Save a PDF/screenshot. **Best email source** | Primary email source |
| **Accounting-software advisor directories** (Xero, MYOB, QuickBooks) | Practices by location, sometimes size | Research only unless terms clearly allow it | Size cross-check; then the website |
| **Local networks** (best source) | CPA Australia, CA ANZ and IPA branch meetings, discussion groups and CPD sessions; local chamber of commerce and council small-business events; the founder's own accountant; independent insurance brokers; MSPs that serve accountants | **Yes, once the person says "send me details"** (express consent). Every AU email needs the same per-contact evidence row as the US (`playbook.md` §3.5), with `country = AU`.

**List target:** 40 AU practices researched by 30 Oct; expect ~15–20 emailable (assumption) plus warm contacts.

## 5. Call script (20–25 minutes, adapted to AU triggers)

**Call hours:** 11:00–15:00 AEDT, Tue–Thu (after the US call block). Not 2 Nov, not 18–25 Nov. Brisbane is an hour behind, Perth three. Offer in person if the practice is local.
**Tone:** calm, plain English. We explain risk; we don't sell fear. Talk 30% of the time or less in discovery.

### 5.1 Opening (1 minute)
> "Thanks for making time, [FIRST_NAME], especially after October. Here's what I'd suggest for 20 minutes: a few questions about how [PRACTICE] handles client data today, then I'll show you what the Pack looks like, and if it fits I'll explain the pilot and the price. If it's not a fit, I'll say so. Sound OK?"

### 5.2 Discovery (8–10 minutes; record the answer type in `notes`, never the details of a weakness)
| # | Question | Listen for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "How do client documents and TFNs reach you today: email, a portal, paper? Who can see them, and how do you get rid of them?" | TFN Rule gaps (storage, access, disposal) |
| 2 | "Does everyone log in to Online services for agents with their **own** myID? Has a login ever been shared, even briefly?" | Shared access; MFA habits |
| 3 | "If a staff laptop was stolen tomorrow, what would you do first? Would you know to tell the ATO?" | Whether a breach response plan exists |
| 4 | "When the TPB's quality-management and supervision obligations started for practices your size in July 2025, what did you put in place? Did it cover IT or data security?" | Documented policies; whether security was included |
| 5 | "Do you use Practice Protect, an IT provider, or nothing formal? What does it cost, and what doesn't it do for you?" | Overlap / gap; price anchor |
| 6 | "Has your PI or cyber insurer asked about MFA, backups or the Essential Eight? What did you send them?" | Insurance side door |
| 7 | "What would a written policy, a breach plan and a fix list be worth to you as a one-off, and in which month could you give it two hours?" | Price anchor; timing |
| 8 | "Would you rather buy this from your IT provider, your professional body or an independent specialist?" | Channel preference (partner learning) |

### 5.3 Explaining the triggers (use only what came up; one or two sentences each)

**TFN Rule (always relevant):**
> "Every tax agent is a TFN recipient. The TFN Rule says TFN information has to be protected, only used for tax purposes, and disposed of securely. And a data breach involving TFNs can be notifiable under the NDB scheme whatever your turnover, so the small-business exemption doesn't cover it. The Pack writes down how [PRACTICE] does that today and what to tighten."
*Don't:* speculate about Privacy Act reform or dates. Don't say they are "non-compliant".

**TPB Code and breach reporting:**
> "Since July 2024, registered agents have to report significant breaches of the Code, their own or another practitioner's, to the TPB within 30 days. Whether a data incident counts depends on the facts, so the breach plan includes a step to make that call quickly, rather than on day 29."
*Don't:* say the TPB requires specific security controls. We found no QMS requirement that names cyber controls. Say "the TPB's guidance recommends MFA and secure portals".

**ATO myID and MFA:**
> "The ATO requires MFA for Online services for agents, and each person needs their own myID. If access is ever compromised, the ATO expects you to tell them straight away. The Evidence Report has a line for exactly that: own myID with MFA for every staff member, marked Verified, Owner-stated or Gap."
*Don't:* quote a start date for the MFA mandate (not verified).

### 5.4 Show, don't scan (3–5 minutes)
- **Default:** show a sample Pack built for a **company-owned demo practice**: policy table of contents, the breach-plan first page, and the one-page Evidence Report.
- **Their own domain (optional):** only if they ask, and only after they reply **in writing from their work address** with the consent text (PRD §7) naming the domain. Then passive checks only (MX, SPF, DKIM they name, DMARC, TLS, headers from one page load), results sent only to that work address. Walk through at most 3 items in plain English. Never mention anything we "noticed" before consent.

### 5.5 Close (3 minutes)
> "Here's the pilot. The Client Data Security Pack is [AU_PILOT_PRICE] plus GST, one-off. You'd be one of two founding pilots before Christmas, so I'll run your intake myself. It's a 60-minute intake at a time that suits you, then within 7 business days you get the policy, the breach plan, the one-page evidence snapshot and your top 5 fixes. In return I'd ask for a 30-minute feedback call. I have [SLOT_1] or [SLOT_2]. Would you like one of them?"

- **Yes:** send the engagement letter and payment link the same day (G-AU2, G-AU6). Book the intake. Stage `7-link-sent`.
- **Not now:** "Would February suit better? I'll put a note in for [DATE]." Stage `nurture-feb`.
- **No:** "Thanks for being straight with me. What would have made it a yes?" Record `lost_reason`.

### 5.6 AU objections (short answers)
| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "We already have Practice Protect." | "Then a lot of the technical side is probably covered. The question is whether you also have a written policy and breach plan in your practice's words, and evidence of what's in place. If you do, you don't need us, and I'd rather tell you that now." (Never describe their product's gaps; ask the prospect.) |
| "The Privacy Act doesn't apply to small businesses." | "Much of it may not at your size. The TFN Rule and breach notification for TFN information apply to every tax agent regardless of turnover. That's what the Pack is built around." (No reform dates.) |
| "My IT guy handles it." | "Good. He fixes things; the Pack writes down what's in place and what's next, and the fix list goes straight to him. He's welcome on the intake." |
| "We're too busy." | "That's why I didn't call before 2 November or in BAS week. The intake is 60 minutes; late November, early December or February?" |
| "Is it TPB-approved or ATO-endorsed?" | "No. Neither body approves products like this. It's built on their published guidance, and you stay responsible for your obligations." |
| "Too expensive." | "What would it cost in your own hours to write a policy and breach plan from scratch? The pilot price is for two practices only, in exchange for feedback." |
| "We're too small to be a target." | "Small practices hold exactly what scammers want: TFNs, bank details and myGov-linked identities. I can't promise the Pack stops an attack; it makes sure you know what's in place and what to do first." |
| "Is AI reliable?" | "I write and check every document myself. AI helps with drafting and with sorting suspicious emails, and a person reviews anything that matters. You get a plan you can read and challenge." |
