**Author:** Head of Market Research · **Date:** 2026-09-26 · **Status:** Draft for founder decision

## 1. Verdict

**Option C (run both, with different jobs).** Keep **US tax/accounting firms as the revenue market** for the PTIN window (mid-Oct to 11 Dec 2026). The US still scores **43/50** after founder access drops to 2. Use **Australian tax/accounting practices for discovery interviews, in-person partner meetings and 1–2 paid pilots** from **3 Nov to 11 Dec 2026**. Australia scores **35/50** because it has no annual attestation like the W-12, an entrenched accountant-specific incumbent (Practice Protect) and about a third of the US SAM. Decide on 15 Feb 2027 whether Australia becomes the 2027 home market.
**Do not choose B now.** Australian tax agents are in peak lodgment season until 2 Nov (individuals) and 25 Nov (Q1 BAS), so switching today loses the only hard, dated trigger we've found in any market and gains no Australian selling time before November.

### 2b. AU tax agent / accounting practices: 35
- **Pain 4.** **Fact:** the Privacy (Tax File Number) Rule 2015 and the Notifiable Data Breaches (NDB) scheme apply to TFN information **regardless of turnover**, so the A$3M small-business exemption does not shield a tax practice's TFN data ([OAIC TFN Rule](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-guidance-for-organisations-and-government-agencies/handling-personal-information/the-privacy-tax-file-number-rule-2015-and-the-protection-of-tax-file-number-information); [TPB NDB page](https://www.tpb.gov.au/notifiable-data-breaches-scheme)). The new TPB Code obligations (quality management, supervision, confidentiality with government) have applied to practices with ≤100 staff since **1 Jul 2025** ([TPB FAQs](https://www.tpb.gov.au/faqs-code-determination); [TPB(GS) 53/2024](https://www.tpb.gov.au/tpb-gs-53-2024-supervision-competency-and-quality-management-under-tax-agent-services-act-2009)). The ATO mandates MFA for Online services for agents ([ATO OSfA T&Cs](https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-and-super-professionals/digital-services/online-services-for-agents/ato-online-services-for-agents-terms-and-conditions?page=1)). Not a 5 because **no rule requires a written security plan and no annual form asks the owner to attest to one**, unlike the W-12.
- **WTP 3.** **Fact:** about 1,500 firms already pay Practice Protect ([Practice Protect overview](https://practiceprotect.com/overview/)). **Fact (stale, 2019):** nearly half of Australian small businesses spent under A$500 a year on cyber security ([ACSC 2023 survey PDF citing 2019](https://www.cyber.gov.au/sites/default/files/2023-03/2023_ACSC_Cyber%20Security%20and%20Australian%20Small%20Businesses%20Survey%20Results_D1.pdf)). Only 20% had a formal cyber policy or staff training in 2025 ([Cyber Wardens Pulse Check 2025](https://cyberwardens.com.au/research-report/small-business-cyber-security-pulse-check-report/)).
- **Reach 4.** **Fact:** the TPB public register is searchable ([TPB register](https://www.tpb.gov.au/public-register)), and there were 46,900 registered tax agents at 30 Jun 2025 ([TPB Annual Report 2024–25](https://www.tpb.gov.au/annual-report)). CPA Australia, CA ANZ and IPA run find-an-accountant directories. Minus 1 because the Spam Act allows B2B cold email only to **conspicuously published** work addresses relevant to the recipient's role ([ACMA](https://www.acma.gov.au/avoid-sending-spam)). That is stricter than CAN-SPAM, which is opt-out.
- **Speed 3.** **Inference:** the owner decides alone, but the season blocks buying. Peak runs through 2 Nov 2026 (individuals) and 25 Nov 2026 (Q1 BAS via agent), then the summer holiday stops activity from mid-Dec to late Jan (**Assumption**, not sourced).
- **Gap 2.** **Fact:** Practice Protect is Australian-born, built for accounting firms, used by 23,000+ accountants, owned by Rightworks since 2024 ([Rightworks](https://www.rightworks.com/news/rightworks-acquires-practice-protect/)), and bundled with a discount on Accountancy Insurance's Cyber Shield policy ([Accountancy Insurance](https://www.accountancyinsurance.com.au/practice-protect/)). Free substitutes also exist: the ASD Cyber Health Check ([cyber.gov.au](https://www.cyber.gov.au/cyberhealthcheck)) and IDCARE's free Small Business Cyber Resilience Service for firms with ≤19 staff ([business.gov.au](https://business.gov.au/grants-and-programs/small-business-cyber-resilience-service)). **Inference:** nobody we found sells a "security plan + evidence + fix list" mapped to TPB/TFN duties. That gap is narrower than in the US, though, because Practice Protect already owns the "security for accountants" story.
- **Founder 4.** Same time zone, local phone number, in-person meetings, and branch events (CPA/IPA/CA ANZ). Rises to 5 if the founder has accounting contacts. **Please confirm.**

### 2c. AU dental: 36
- **Pain 4.** **Fact:** health service providers are covered by the Privacy Act **regardless of turnover** ([OAIC health privacy guide](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-guidance-for-organisations-and-government-agencies/health-service-providers/guide-to-health-privacy)). Health was the top NDB sector in 2025 with 225 of 1,205 notifications ([OAIC, 2025 stats](https://www.oaic.gov.au/news/media-centre/data-breach-notifications-increase-to-all-time-high-in-2025,-new-ndb-stats-show); [Privacy 108](https://privacy108.com.au/insights/data-breach-reporting-july-december-2025/)). There's no dated annual trigger.
- **WTP 3, Reach 4, Speed 3, Gap 3** (**Inference**): 20,919 dental businesses (2026) ([IBISWorld](https://www.ibisworld.com/australia/number-of-businesses/dental-services/613/)). The Australian Dental Association and Google Maps make practices findable. Practices depend on dental practice-software vendors and IT MSPs. We found no dental equivalent of Practice Protect (not researched in depth).

### 2d. AU general practice: 35 and AU law: 34
- **GP pain 5:** accreditation against RACGP Standards includes Criterion C6.4 Information security ([RACGP C6.4](https://www.racgp.org.au/running-a-practice/practice-standards/standards-5th-edition/standards-for-general-practices-5th-ed/core-standards/core-standard-6/criterion-c6-4-information-security)). The **6th edition was released 26 Aug 2026 with stronger cyber and digital-governance requirements; the transition date has not been announced** ([MedAssure, 2026](https://medassure.com.au/resources/racgp-6th-edition-standards/); [EMPHN](https://emphn.org.au/news/racgp-releases-standards-for-general-practices-6th-edition/)). **Speed 2 and Gap 2:** practice managers, owner-GPs and PHNs are all involved, and PHNs give free digital-health support. **Watch this one:** once the transition date is set it becomes a dated trigger.
- **Law pain 4:** business email compromise (BEC) trust-account fraud is the most common cyber claim notified to LPLC ([LPLC](https://lplc.com.au/risk-advice/technology-cyber/cyber-security)). **Reach 3:** there were 16,793 private law practices in Oct 2024, and **78% were sole practices** ([2024 National Profile of Solicitors](https://www.lawsociety.com.au/sites/default/files/2025-06/2024%20National%20Profile%20of%20Solicitors%20-%20Final.pdf)), so the 5–50 staff ICP is small.

### 2e. Tie-break: why AU accounting over AU dental for discovery
**Inference.** AU dental is 1 point higher, but AU accounting practices are the **same buyer persona** as our US segment. The interview script, messaging spine and Pack structure transfer with a content swap, and 10 interviews across two countries on one persona teach us more than 5 + 5 on two personas. Revisit if AU accounting interviews show the "Practice Protect already covers it" objection in 3+ of the first 6.

| Sydney / Melbourne / Canberra / Hobart (AEDT, UTC+11) | 06:00–09:00 = **ET 15:00–18:00** / **PT 12:00–15:00** (previous day) | 07:00–10:00 = **ET 15:00–18:00** / **PT 12:00–15:00** | ~3 h with ET afternoons; ~5 h with PT (06:00–11:00 Sydney = PT 11:00–16:00 after Nov 1) |

| Perth (AWST, UTC+8) | 21:00–23:00 = ET 09:00–11:00 **same day** | 21:00–23:00 = ET 08:00–10:00 | Evening calls reach US mornings |

**Implications:** (1) US Monday is Tuesday morning in Sydney; US Friday afternoon is Saturday morning. (2) Change the playbook's "Tue/Thu 10–12 local to the prospect" call blocks to **12:00–15:00 PT / 15:00–17:00 ET**, which is **07:00–10:00 Sydney**. (3) **Prefer Pacific and Mountain focus states** (e.g. CA, WA, OR, AZ, CO, NV) for `[STATE_1–3]`, because their whole afternoon is reachable. **Inference:** workable, but early starts every weekday for 9 weeks carry a real burnout risk for a solo founder.

### 5e. Spam Act applies to US cold email too
**Fact:** a message has an "Australian link", and so falls under the Spam Act, if the sender is **physically present in Australia** when it is sent ([Spam Act s7, AustLII](https://austlii.edu.au/cgi-bin/viewdoc/au/legis/cth/consol_act/sa200366/s7.html)). **So every US email from our founder must also meet the Spam Act:** send only to work addresses **conspicuously published** by the firm and relevant to the recipient's role, with accurate sender identification and a working unsubscribe ([ACMA](https://www.acma.gov.au/avoid-sending-spam)). **No guessed or pattern-generated addresses.** The current playbook already prefers firm-website addresses. Legal-ops should confirm there are no other gaps. ACMA has issued large spam penalties ([DLA Piper, 2024](https://privacymatters.dlapiper.com/2024/08/australias-e-marketing-expectations-when-customers-dont-give-a-spam/)).

## 8. Risks and what would change the verdict

1. **US execution risk from abroad.** If fewer than 6 US calls are held by 6 Nov (playbook Checkpoint 1) **and** AU interviews convert 2+ pilots, move to **B** for 2027.
2. **Founder network.** If the founder has 10+ warm Australian accounting contacts, AU founder access becomes 5 and reach becomes 5 → **38**. That makes AU a strong market and moves the choice toward C weighted 50/50.

5. **Practice Protect overlap.** If 3 or more of the first 6 AU owners say Practice Protect or their MSP "already covers" it, AU accounting drops to Gap 1 and AU dental becomes the AU lead.

## 10. Open questions to validate with customers (AU interviews)
