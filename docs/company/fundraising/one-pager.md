**What we do.** ChimeraShield is a plain-English security advisor for 5–50 person professional firms with no IT staff. Our first product helps US tax and accounting firms produce a **firm-specific written information security plan (WISP)**, a one-page **Evidence Report** (each control marked Verified, Owner-stated or Gap) and a **Fix-First plan** of the top 5 gaps, delivered within a week. A monthly **Watch** subscription keeps the evidence current and gives staff an "Is this safe?" helpdesk during tax season.

**The problem.** Every paid tax preparer attests at PTIN renewal (Form W-12, line 11) that they know they must keep a WISP, and the FTC Safeguards Rule applies to tax preparers at any size (Fact). Small firms choose between a free or $29–$999 template they have to complete alone and managed IT priced per user (Fact, vendor pages). *Hypothesis to test:* most small firms never finish the template and can't show what's actually in place.

**Why now.** The Safeguards breach-notice rule has been in force since May 2024 (Fact). The IRS logged ~300 tax-practice breaches in H1 2025, and IRS-themed phishing aimed at tax preparers hit ~10,000 orgs in Feb 2026 (Fact, secondary sources). LLMs make a firm-specific plan cheap to draft, with a human reviewing every deliverable.

**Market.** ~14,960 reachable US accounting/tax firms with 5–49 staff → SAM **$8.8M–$26.7M/yr** at $49–$149/mo. Dental, our planned second segment on the same engine, adds **$28M–$86M** (Assumptions on size bands and reach; counts from Census-derived sources, some stale). Accounting alone is a small-business market; the larger opportunity depends on carrying the engine to other regulated verticals (untested).

**Competition.** Guardz ($84M raised), Coro, Huntress and Microsoft already sell AI-assisted security to SMBs, mostly **through MSPs** and built for IT operators (Fact). None produces an owner-facing, evidence-labeled WISP. Free scanners and $29 templates are the low-end substitutes. Our wedge: **no MSP, no agent, plain English, shaped around a dated compliance trigger.**
