# Monthly Investor / Advisor Update: Template

**Owner:** fundraising-advisor · **Date:** 2026-09-24
**Send:** by the 3rd business day of each month, to 3–10 advisors, potential angels and helpful friends. Start **1 Nov 2026** (covering October), even though there are no investors yet.

**Why bother pre-traction:** a run of honest monthly updates is the best evidence a solo founder can give of execution. When you do ask for money, the people reading it will have watched you hit (or miss and fix) targets for 6+ months.

**Rules**
- Same structure every month, so readers can compare.
- Report bad months with the same care as good ones. Never round up; never count "verbal yes" as a customer.
- Label projections. Don't name customers or quote them without written permission.
- Short: aim for a 3-minute read. Put details in an optional appendix.
- Include an opt-out line.

## Seasonal notes to include when relevant
- **Oct–Dec:** PTIN window. Lead with packs sold, Watch attach and the annual-prepay share.
- **Jan–Apr:** no new accounting sales by design. Lead with helpdesk usage, cancellations, dental progress and product work. Say this upfront so a quiet sales line doesn't read as failure.
- **May–Jun:** the retention test. Report May cancellations explicitly, even if bad; this is the number future investors will care about most.

## First issue (1 Nov 2026): what should be in it

- Whether the entity, domain, payments and insurance are live (or what's blocking them).
- First Readiness Checks and first paid pack (or honestly: none yet, and why).
- The 15 Dec targets from `pitch-outline.md` slide 9B, so readers can hold you to them.
