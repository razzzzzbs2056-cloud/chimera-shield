## 1. Positioning (adapted from `competitors/smb-landscape-2026.md` §4)

> **For owners of 5–50 person tax, CPA and accounting firms with no in-house IT who must attest to a written security plan every PTIN renewal,** ChimeraShield is the plain-English, done-with-you service that **writes a WISP describing your actual firm, shows which safeguards are verified, and tells you the 5 things to fix first**, unlike free or $29–$999 WISP templates, which you still fill in alone and which prove nothing, or MSP bundles priced per user, which need an IT company to run them.

Changes from the landscape statement: the segment is narrowed, the trigger (PTIN/W-12) is added, and the deliverable is the WISP plus evidence instead of "checks your email and domain". Our own research says the domain check is a free commodity, so it is a supporting feature and never the headline.

## 2. One-liner

**Primary (for bios, email signatures, directory listings):**
> ChimeraShield helps small tax and accounting firms get a written security plan that matches what is really in place, with proof and a short fix list, in plain English, before PTIN renewal.

**Short form (under 12 words):**
> A WISP that fits your firm, with proof, before PTIN renewal.

## 3. Value proposition

> **Check the WISP box on your PTIN renewal and mean it.** In about two hours of your time, we build a written information security plan around how your firm actually works, show you which safeguards we could verify and which are still gaps, and give you the 5 fixes that matter most, in words you can hand to your office manager. You sign it as your firm's Qualified Individual. We don't give legal advice, and we never ask for client data.

The "about two hours" is the PRD's 60-min intake + 30-min debrief + a 15-min intake checklist (PRD §4, AC3). Re-measure after the first 3 packs.

## 4. Headline rules
- **Lead with the deadline and the proof.** The PTIN renewal window and the W-12 attestation are the reason to act now. Evidence ("Verified / Owner-stated / Gap") is the reason to pick us over a template.
- **No AI in any headline, subject line or hero.** AI shows up only in the FAQ, where we explain honestly how we use it (drafting help, human review). The landscape doc shows "AI-native" is not a differentiator and every competitor claims it.
- **No fear.** Name the obligation calmly. Never lead with breaches, hackers or fines.

**Approved headline options (test in this order):**
1. *Before you renew your PTIN, find the 3 gaps in your security plan.* (readiness check, primary)
2. *Your W-12 asks about your security plan. Can you show it?* (readiness check, variant)
3. *A security plan that describes your firm, with proof, before December 31.* (Pack)

## 7. Objections and answers

| Objection (our hypothesis until interviews confirm) | Answer |
|---|---|
| "The IRS template is free. Why pay?" | "It is free, and it's a good starting point. Most of the work is filling in 28 pages about your own systems. | "I bought a $29 WISP template already." | "Then you have the structure. The question is whether it describes your firm today and whether you could show someone it's true. The free check will tell you in about 10 minutes whether the Pack is worth it for you." |

| "The FTC isn't going to come after a 10-person firm." | "We can't tell you the odds, and we won't try to scare you. You still attest on the W-12 each year, and insurers and clients increasingly ask. A plan that matches reality also makes you faster if something ever does go wrong." |
| "We're small, so it doesn't apply." | "Part of it may not. If you hold information on fewer than 5,000 consumers (everyone whose records you keep, including past years), four items may be excused under 16 CFR 314.6. | "It's almost filing season. I have no time." | "That's why the timing is now. About two hours of your time in October or November, delivered within a week, and you're not thinking about it in February." |

## 8. Words to use / words to avoid

| Use | Avoid | Why |
|---|---|---|
| written security plan (WISP) | "cybersecurity posture", "security program maturity" | Use their form's language. |
| PTIN renewal, W-12, line 11 | "compliance deadline" (vague) | The concrete trigger. |
| your firm, your office, your staff | "organization", "enterprise", "endpoint" | They run a practice. |
| Qualified Individual (with a one-line explanation) | "CISO", "security officer" | The rule's own term. |
| Verified / Owner-stated / Gap | "compliant", "passes", "certified", "approved", "audit-ready", "IRS-approved" | We never certify compliance (PRD AC10). Liability risk. |
| "helps you meet", "structured on Pub 5708", "mapped to the Safeguards Rule" | "guarantees compliance", "makes you compliant", "official", "IRS-endorsed" | Truthful and not affiliated. |
| fix first, 5 fixes, plain English | "comprehensive", "end-to-end", "holistic" | Short and concrete. |
| about two hours of your time; done in a week | "set it and forget it", "effortless" | Honest effort estimate. |
| multi-factor authentication (a second step when you log in) | "MFA" alone on first use | Jargon needs a one-line explanation. |
| suspicious email, fake IRS email | "threat actors", "cyber criminals are targeting you", "hackers" | Calm, not fear. |
| "we don't give legal advice" | "our experts will protect you" | Honest scope. |
| (none in headlines) | "AI-powered", "AI-native", "agentic", "next-gen", "military-grade" | Every competitor says it; our buyer doesn't buy it. |
| "we can't promise nothing will go wrong" | "breach-proof", "bulletproof", "never worry again" | No security guarantees (brand rule). |
| "you and our reviewer", "our service providers process it under contract", "we never sell it" | "only you", "never shared", "no one else sees it" | A person reviews every result and subprocessors handle it (legal review M1–M3). |
| "fewer than 5,000 **consumers**", "may be excused", "may not apply" | "fewer than 5,000 clients", "doesn't apply to you" | The rule counts consumers, including past years, and exemption depends on facts (M4–M5). |
| "we never ask for client data" | "no client data, ever" | We can't fully control screen-share accidents (M12). |
| "Safeguards WISP & Evidence Pack" | "Safeguards-Ready" | May imply certification (M9). |

**Don't imply government affiliation.** No IRS logos, seals or "IRS" in our domain or product name. Several WISP template sites use PTIN/IRS-style domains (search results, 2026-09-24). That's their choice. We stand out by being clearly independent.
