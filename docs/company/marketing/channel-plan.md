| Qualified conversation → Pack bought | 10–20% | Assumption. Tested by the PRD kill rule (0 from 30 = pivot) |

**Working backward:** 3 Packs ≈ 60 completed checks at 5% **or** 15–30 qualified conversations. We need both paths running. Target by 1 Dec: **60 completed checks and 30 qualified conversations**, about 60% from cold email and 40% from LinkedIn.

A **qualified conversation** means a live call or a written back-and-forth with an owner/partner/manager of a 5–50 person firm, about their WISP, where they told us at least one fact about their situation.

**Sequence** (outreach-writing skill; stop on any reply or opt-out):

   > **Subject:** question about [firm]'s wisp
   >
   > Hi [FIRST_NAME],
   >
   > PTIN renewal opens in mid-October, and the W-12 asks every preparer to confirm they know they need a written security plan. I saw [FIRM] on [SOURCE: NAEA directory / state CPA society] and wondered how you handle yours.
   >
   > I built a free 12-question check that tells a firm its 3 biggest gaps before renewal. Want me to send the link?
   >
   > [FOUNDER_NAME], Founder, ChimeraShield Pty Ltd (ABN [ABN])
   > [FOUNDER_NAME]@[DOMAIN] · [POSTAL_ADDRESS]
   >
   > Not relevant? Reply "unsubscribe" (or just "no") and I won't email you again.

   (Footer per `legal/spam-act-cold-email.md` §4. The live sequence and kit are owned by sales: `sales/sequences.md`, `ads/cold-email-launch-kit.md`. If they differ, theirs wins.)
2. **+3 days:** short bump adding one useful fact (e.g. the 5,000-consumer exemption in one sentence).
3. **+5 days:** the resource. The check link, plus a link to the free IRS Pub 5708.
4. **+7 days:** "closing the loop". No more emails after this.

**Compliance (CAN-SPAM and, because we send from Australia, the Spam Act 2003):** accurate sender and subject; the authorising entity and ABN; real postal address; working unsubscribe for at least 30 days, honoured within 5 business days (the stricter Spam Act limit; we honour it the same day). Only addresses the firm or person published themselves, where the message relates to their role, with the source recorded as consent evidence. No bought lists. Keep a suppression list and apply it to both domains and to the readiness-check emails.

## 5. Kill / scale rules

Checkpoints: **Fri 30 Oct** (2 weeks after the check goes live) and **Fri 20 Nov**. Decide on numbers, not feelings.

### Channel 1: LinkedIn
| Signal at checkpoint | Decision |
|---|---|
| **Scale:** ≥ 8 checks **or** ≥ 2 Pack fit calls from LinkedIn since 15 Oct | Add 20 DMs/week, turn the 2 best posts into a carousel and a comment template, and ask every check-taker "who else should see this?" |
| **Hold:** 3–7 checks, some conversations | Keep volume. Change the post hook and the DM opener, and re-test for 2 weeks. |
| **Kill (reduce):** < 3 checks **and** < 3 qualified conversations after ≥ 200 ICP connection requests | Drop to 1 post a week and move the freed time to channel 2 or to warm referrals. |

### Channel 2: Cold email
| Signal | Decision |
|---|---|
| **Pause immediately (health):** bounce rate > 3% in any week, any spam-folder placement in seed tests, or the Google Postmaster spam rate climbing toward 0.3% | Stop sending. Clean the list, check SPF/DKIM/DMARC, and cut volume by half for a week. (Google's sender guidelines say to keep the user-reported spam rate below 0.3%: [Google Workspace Admin Help](https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126).) |
| **Scale:** ≥ 3% positive-reply rate **and** ≥ 1 Pack fit call, after ≥ 300 delivered first touches | Add a second mailbox on the secondary domain and grow to 40–50/day in total, keeping each mailbox at or under 30/day. |
| **Hold:** 1.5–3% positive replies | Test a new first line and subject for 150 sends. |
| **Kill:** < 1.5% positive replies after ≥ 400 delivered first touches, with healthy deliverability | Stop cold email. Move the time to LinkedIn and warm referrals. |

### Whole plan (from PRD §9, restated)
- **0 Packs sold from ≥ 30 qualified conversations by 1 Dec 2026,** or 3+ of the first 6 accounting interviews anchor WISP value under $100 → trigger the self-serve $79–$149 pivot (catalog §5). Channels keep running; the offer changes.
- **Check completions but no Pack calls** (≥ 40 checks, 0 calls by 20 Nov) → the result email is the problem. Rewrite the Pack offer in it and add a founder-voice reply-to-book line.

## 6. Name and domain note

**Don't claim or assume any of these are available.** The founder must check availability at a registrar, and run the 15-minute name screen in `legal/launch-checklist.md` 1.1 (USPTO classes 9 and 42, Google, LinkedIn) first. "Chimera" and "Shield" are both common in security names, so if the screen turns up conflicts, rename now while it's cheap.

| Role | Option | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Main site (1st choice) | `chimerashield.com` | Matches the company name. Hosts `/check` and `/wisp-pack`. |
| Main site (2nd choice) | `chimerashield.co` | Only if the .com is taken by an unrelated business. Check that nobody is using the .com in security first (a confusion risk). |
| Main site (3rd choice) | `trychimerashield.com` | Common startup pattern. Fine for a site, a little weaker for trust. |
| **Cold-email secondary domain** | `getchimerashield.com` | Per the outreach-writing skill. Used **only** for cold email so any reputation hit doesn't reach the main domain. Redirect its website to the main domain, and give it its own SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Every email still says "ChimeraShield Pty Ltd" and gives the real sender name, ABN and postal address. |

**Avoid:** anything containing "irs", "ptin", "5708" or "gov". It could look like a government site, and we'd look like the template sellers we want to stand apart from.

**Email setup:** `hello@`, `[FOUNDER_NAME]@` and `security@` on the main domain; `[FOUNDER_NAME]@` on the secondary domain. MFA on all admin accounts. DMARC starts at `p=none` with reports, then moves to `quarantine` once reports are clean. As a security company, our own domain has to pass our own readiness check.
