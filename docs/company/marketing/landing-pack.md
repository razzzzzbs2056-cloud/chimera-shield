## Why a template isn't enough

The IRS publishes a free WISP template, Publication 5708. It's a good starting point, and if you have the time to finish it yourself, you should.

Most owners don't struggle with the template. They struggle with the questions it asks about their own firm: which systems hold client data, who has access, whether MFA is really on everywhere, what their software vendors are responsible for. And a finished template still can't tell you which of those answers you could actually show someone.

The Pack does that part with you.

## What you get

**1. Your firm-specific WISP**
Structured on IRS Pub 5708 and mapped section by section to the FTC Safeguards Rule (16 CFR 314.4). It names your people, your systems and your service providers. If your firm has fewer than 5,000 consumers, the items excused under 16 CFR 314.6 are marked with the reason. You sign it as Qualified Individual, with an annual review date.

**2. A one-page Evidence Report**
At least 8 safeguards, including MFA on email and tax software, device encryption, email security settings, backups, training, service-provider oversight and your incident contact. Each is marked:
- **Verified:** we saw it (a public DNS record, or a setting you showed us on screen-share)
- **Owner-stated:** you told us, and we didn't observe it
- **Gap:** not in place yet

Use it when an insurer, a client or a partner asks what you have in place. It never says "compliant". It says what's true.

**3. Your Fix-First plan**
Exactly 5 gaps, ranked by risk and effort. Each one says what to do, why it matters (one sentence, no jargon), who can do it, and roughly how long it takes, with a vendor-neutral how-to link.

## FAQ

**How is this different from the free IRS template?**
The template is the structure. We fill it in with you from your firm's real facts, check what we can, mark what we couldn't check, and rank what to fix. You end up with a plan you understand and can show someone.

**Will this make my firm compliant?**
We can't promise that, and nobody honest can. The Pack helps you document and improve your safeguards. You sign the plan as Qualified Individual and remain responsible for compliance. We don't give legal advice.

**Do you need access to our systems or client data?**
No. We never log in. You show us settings on a screen-share. We never collect taxpayer information, so please don't share any.

**What do you check on our domain?**
Only public records for your own domain, with your written permission: email-security settings (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), mail servers, your website certificate and basic security headers. No scans, no login attempts, no test phishing.

**We have fewer than 5,000 clients. Do we still need a WISP?**
Yes. The written plan, MFA, encryption and training still apply. Four items are excused under 16 CFR 314.6, and your WISP marks them.

**What if we already have an IT company?**
If they maintain a current WISP for you, you probably don't need the Pack. If they handle computers but not the plan, the Pack gives them a clear list of what to fix.

**How long does it take?**
About two hours of your time. Delivery within 7 business days of the intake call.
