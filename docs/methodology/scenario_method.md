# Scenario method

Ten mandatory scenarios (`scenarios/`): S01 Middle East conflict · S02 India trade/transit disruption ·
S03 US–China fragmentation · S04 global recession · S05 severe Himalayan flooding · S06 migration
restrictions · S07 rapid AI adoption · S08 LDC-graduation preference changes · S09 regional electricity
market transformation · S10 compound shocks (S01+S02+S04+S05).

For each: hypothetical trigger; countries and institutions; exposure references (verified exposure comes
from the register); transmission channels; shocks (magnitude `null` until evidenced); household, regional,
sectoral and fiscal impact dimensions; responses; second-order effects; uncertainty; limitations; and
submission to both auditors. Scenarios are not forecasts and carry no probabilities.

Engine statuses: `QUALITATIVE_ONLY` (no quantified shock) · `PARTIAL` (some shocks applied) ·
`QUANTIFIED` (all applied with evidence) · `ILLUSTRATIVE` (at least one shock is an unevidenced sensitivity).
Compound composition applies identical shocks once and refuses to sum conflicting ones.
