# Concept review

Run `bld-reviewer`, then check yourself:
1. **Idea**: can it be stated in one sentence, and does the plan, section and façade express it?
2. **Coordination**: grid, heights, services space and structural depth consistent.
3. **Numbers**: every figure traces to a data file, the tool, or an assumption.
4. **Safety**: egress, fire, structure, access addressed or listed as open.
5. **Buildability**: sequence and temporary works plausible.
6. **Honesty**: disclaimers, scope of carbon figures, unchecked items listed.

Output a ranked findings list with fixes.
