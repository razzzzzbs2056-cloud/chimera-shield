# Market hunting playbook

The goal is not a pretty report. It's one decision: **which narrow segment we sell to first, and where exactly those buyers are.**

## 1. Segment scoring rubric (score each 1–5)

| Criterion | Weight | 5 looks like |
|-----------|--------|--------------|
| Pain intensity | ×3 | Regulated data, recent breaches in the segment, insurance or compliance pressure |
| Willingness to pay | ×2 | Already pays for IT/security or compliance tools; data loss has a clear dollar cost |
| Reachability | ×2 | Public directories, associations, active online communities, clear job titles |
| Sales speed | ×1 | Owner decides alone, no procurement, buys in under 2 weeks |
| Competition gap | ×1 | Incumbents are enterprise-priced or not AI-native in this niche |
| Founder access | ×1 | Founder has contacts, language, or domain knowledge here |

Maximum score is 50. Treat 38 or more as a strong first market. Show the table with a one-line reason per score.

## 2. Evidence standards
- Every number gets a source link and a year. Numbers older than 2 years are marked **stale**.
- Label each claim as **Fact** (sourced), **Assumption** (explain it), or **Inference**.
- Prefer primary sources: government statistics (e.g. US Census County Business Patterns, BLS), regulators (HHS breach portal, ICO), industry associations, and vendor pricing pages.
- Bottom-up sizing: `# businesses in segment × % reachable × annual price`.

## 3. Buying triggers to look for
Compliance audits (HIPAA, PCI, SOC 2, FTC Safeguards Rule for tax preparers), cyber-insurance questionnaires or renewals, a recent phishing or ransomware incident, fundraising due diligence, headcount crossing ~10, and breaches at peers in local news.

## 4. Where buyers live (verify per segment)
- **Directories:** state bar listings, state CPA society directories, dental and medical association member finders, Google Maps categories
- **Communities:** r/smallbusiness, r/msp, r/sysadmin, r/accounting, r/Dentistry, r/LawFirm, industry Facebook and LinkedIn groups
- **Events:** local chamber of commerce, association conferences, IT/MSP meetups
- **Partners:** MSPs, bookkeepers and CPAs, cyber-insurance brokers, practice-management software vendors

## 5. Report format
1. **Verdict** (3 lines): the recommended first segment and why
2. Scoring table
3. Market size (show the math)
4. Buying triggers and where to find buyers (named communities, directories, events)
5. Risks and what would change the verdict
6. Top 3 implications for ChimeraShield
7. Questions to validate in customer interviews
8. Sources
