# Outreach writing

## Structure (under 100 words)
1. **Personal first line**: something true and specific about them, such as a trigger event, a recent post, or their niche.
2. **Problem in their words**: one sentence, using customer-interview language where possible.
3. **Credible reason for writing**: research (discovery phase) or a specific result (sales phase).
4. **One small ask**: "Worth a 15-minute chat next week?" Never ask for a demo and a call and a signup in the same message.
5. **Signature**: real name, company, and `[FOUNDER_NAME]@[DOMAIN]` until the email exists.
6. **Opt-out**: "If this isn't relevant, just reply 'no' and I won't follow up."

## Subject lines
Short and lowercase-friendly, with no clickbait, e.g. "question about [firm]'s IT", "security at small CPA firms".

## Sequence
Touch 1 → +3 days: short bump adding one new piece of value → +5 days: a helpful resource (checklist, free domain check) → +7 days: breakup ("closing the loop"). Stop immediately on any reply or opt-out.

## Compliance
- **CAN-SPAM (US):** accurate sender and subject, physical postal address, working opt-out honored within 10 business days
- **GDPR / UK PECR:** B2B only, with a legitimate-interest basis; no personal-address lists; honor objections
- **CASL (Canada):** implied consent needed (e.g. a publicly listed business email relevant to their role)
- Never buy personal email lists. Never spoof or hide the sender.

## Deliverability for a new domain
- Consider sending cold email from a secondary domain (e.g. `getchimerashield.com`) to protect the main domain's reputation.
- Set up SPF, DKIM, and DMARC before sending. ChimeraShield checks these for customers, so our own must be perfect.
- Warm up: 5–10 emails/day in week 1, rising to about 30/day by week 4. Send plain text, with no links in the first touch.
