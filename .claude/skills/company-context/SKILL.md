## Brand voice
- Calm, plain English, and helpful. We explain risk; we don't sell fear.
- Short sentences. No jargon without a one-line explanation.
- Honest about what the product can and can't do. No security guarantees.

## Hard rules for anything sent in the company's name
- Never invent customers, testimonials, logos, certifications (e.g. "SOC 2 compliant"), or statistics.
- Leave every `TODO` or placeholder visible. Never make up an email address, phone number, or domain.
- Outreach must identify the sender honestly and include an opt-out line. Because we send from Australia, the **Spam Act 2003** applies: email only addresses the firm or person published themselves, where the message relates to their role; include the ABN and an unsubscribe option honoured within 5 business days. See `docs/company/legal/spam-act-cold-email.md`.
- Only scan assets the owner has authorized in writing.
