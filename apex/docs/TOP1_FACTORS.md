# What top performers have in common, and how Apex trains each factor

"Top 1%" is not a scientific category. What research *does* show is a set of factors that
repeatedly predict better health, wealth, performance and longevity. Apex tracks every one of them
and turns them into daily orders.

**Evidence strength:** 🟢 strong (large studies or meta-analyses) · 🟡 moderate or mixed · ⚪ practitioner wisdom (military and coaching practice, not tested in trials)

## The factors

| # | Factor | Evidence | What the research found | Trained in |
|---|--------|:--------:|-------------------------|------------|
| 1 | **Self-control** | 🟢 | In a 32-year study of about 1,000 children, childhood self-control predicted adult health, finances, substance problems and crime on a gradient. It held even between siblings, and children who improved their self-control did better than predicted. [PNAS 2011](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1010076108) | Code & Ownership (temptation resisted, word kept), Boot Camp demerits, Finance |
| 2 | **Conscientiousness** (organised, dependable, hard-working) | 🟢 | The only Big Five trait linked to job performance in every occupation studied (Barrick & Mount 1991, r ≈ .22). In the Terman study it predicted lower mortality (hazard ratio 0.82). [Ann. Behav. Med. 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19455378/) | Boot Camp orders, Habits, Goals |
| 3 | **Grit** (perseverance toward long-term goals) | 🟡 | Predicted West Point retention better than SAT scores or class rank. Across outcomes it explains about 4% of variance: real, but modest. [JPSP 2007](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17547490/) | Code & Ownership (grit check, hard thing), Goals, Operation Iron 75 |
| 4 | **Deliberate practice** | 🟡 | Explains about 26% of performance variance in games, 21% in music, 18% in sport, 4% in education and under 1% in professions. It matters, but it isn't everything. [Psych. Science 2014](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/0956797614535810) | Learning (skill practice), PT drills, Deep Work |
| 5 | **If-then planning** | 🟢 | Across 94 studies, "if X then I do Y" plans had an effect of d ≈ 0.65 on actually attaining goals. [Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2006](https://psycnet.apa.org/record/2007-19538-002) | Boot Camp timed orders, Goals Top 3, Habits |
| 6 | **Aerobic fitness** | 🟢 | Among 122,007 adults, the elite-fitness group had about 80% lower mortality than the least fit, with no upper limit of benefit. [JAMA Netw Open 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30646252/) | PT Test (run), Fitness |
| 7 | **Strength training** | 🟢 | Any resistance training is linked to about 15% lower all-cause mortality. The benefit peaks around 60 minutes a week, and it adds up with cardio. [AJPM 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35599175/) | PT Test (push-ups, pull-ups), Fitness |
| 8 | **Daily movement** | 🟢 | Risk of death falls as daily steps rise, levelling off around 8–10k steps under age 60 and 6–8k over 60. [Lancet Public Health 2022](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanpub/article/PIIS2468-2667(21)00302-9/fulltext) | Fitness (steps) |
| 9 | **Sleep** | 🟢 | Consistent, sufficient sleep underpins memory, mood, metabolism and performance (*Why We Sleep* and the sleep-medicine consensus). | Sleep, Boot Camp (lights out / reveille) |
| 10 | **Nutrition** | 🟢 | Whole foods, enough protein and little alcohol (the dietary-guideline consensus). | Nutrition |
| 11 | **Strong relationships** | 🟢 | Across 148 studies (308k people), strong social ties meant about 50% higher odds of survival, comparable to smoking or inactivity as a risk factor. [PLOS Med 2010](https://journals.plos.org/plosmedicine/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pmed.1000316) | Relationships |
| 12 | **Stress control** (breathing) | 🟡 | In a randomised trial, five minutes a day of exhale-focused breathing improved mood more than meditation did (state anxiety down about 17%). [Cell Rep. Med. 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36630953/) | Boot Camp box breathing, Mindset |
| 13 | **Mental toughness: the "Big Four"** | ⚪🟡 | Navy SEAL training teaches micro-goals, visualisation, self-talk and arousal control. Reportedly, pass rates improved after it was introduced. [SEALFIT](https://sealfit.com/the-big-4-of-mental-toughness/) | Boot Camp (Big Four card) |
| 14 | **Delayed gratification** | 🟡 | The famous marshmallow effect shrank a lot in a larger replication once family background was controlled for. Train self-control as an adult; don't treat it as destiny. [Watts 2018](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/publications/observer/obsonline/a-new-approach-to-the-marshmallow-test-yields-complex-findings.html) | Code & Ownership |
| 15 | **Ownership / internal locus of control** | ⚪ | *Extreme Ownership* (Willink & Babin): own outcomes, without blaming anyone. | Code & Ownership |
| 16 | **Deep focus** | ⚪🟡 | *Deep Work*, and research on the cost of attention switching. | Deep Work, Digital Discipline |
| 17 | **Money behaviour** (save, invest, spend below your means) | 🟢 | *The Psychology of Money*. Saving rate and time in the market drive compounding. | Finance |
| 18 | **Continuous learning** | 🟡 | Retrieval and spaced practice beat re-reading (*Make It Stick*). | Learning |
| 19 | **Reflection** (After Action Review) | ⚪🟡 | The US Army AAR format asks: what was planned, what happened, why, and what to sustain or improve. | Boot Camp AAR, Daily Review |
| 20 | **Environment and order** | ⚪ | Make your bed and lay out your gear: small wins that cut down on decisions (*Make Your Bed*, *Atomic Habits*). | Boot Camp orders, Habits |

## How Apex turns this into military-style training

- **Boot Camp HQ**: timed daily orders from reveille to lights out. Orders linked to other trackers tick themselves off automatically. Late orders count half and missed orders earn demerits. It also includes a drill sergeant, the "Big Four" drills, and an After Action Review.
- **Operation Iron 75**: a 75-day program. In strict mode, a single failed day resets you to day 1.
- **PT Test**: a military-style test with 4 events scored 0–100 by age and sex, plus a daily drill prescription generated from your last test.
- **Code & Ownership**: owning mistakes, the daily hard thing (with a "cookie jar" of past ones), keeping your word, no complaining, cold exposure, temptations resisted, core values, and a weekly grit check.
- **Ranks**: every point of Life Score is 1 XP, counted over a rolling 365 days. Ranks run from Recruit to General, so you're demoted if you stop showing up.
- **AI drill sergeant**: the `apex-drill-sergeant` Claude skill issues morning orders and runs your evening debrief, using your real data through the MCP server.

## Safety

Hard is not the same as harmful. Apex never rewards skipping sleep, starving, or training through pain.
The PT tracker stops the drill when you report pain. For medical, mental-health or financial decisions, talk to a professional.
