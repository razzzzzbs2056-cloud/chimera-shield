Precedent gallery · houses in 3D · 1567 to 2005

Houses that changed architecture

Twelve homes, from Palladio's Villa Rotonda to Nishizawa's Moriyama House, modelled in the round. Each stands with a 1.75 m figure for scale, and each card ends with the lesson our own designs took from it.

Interpretive massing models from general knowledge: forms and proportions are recognisable, dimensions are approximate, and details are simplified. Not measured drawings. Facts on the cards should be verified before citing.
