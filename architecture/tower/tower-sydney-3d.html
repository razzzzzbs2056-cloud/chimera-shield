A 30-storey tower for Sydney

Built from the divisions' own outputs: the structural scheme and member sizes, the architects' typical floor of eight apartments around a central core, the foundation study and the independent check. The sun is computed for Sydney's latitude, so north light and winter shadows are real for any date and hour.

Concept feasibility only. No lot has been chosen: the surrounding buildings are illustrative, and Sydney's wind and earthquake values are still to be retrieved and cited by the Codes division. Not for construction.
